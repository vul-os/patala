Open source · non-custodial · no token

Move value.
Hold none of it.

patala is a sovereign, centreless payment substrate — one interface to move money, fiat or crypto, that any product can vendor and self-host. The platform holds no funds, takes no cut, and no one owns the network. Patala is Sesotho for "to pay."

Holds no funds

Non-custodial by construction. Value moves over the rails directly — patala is the interface, never the account.

Takes no cut

No platform fee, no spread, no rent. You pay the rail its own cost and nothing to the middle.

Centreless

No one owns the network. Anyone can vendor it into their own product and self-host it — there is no operator to depend on.

Any rail

One honest interface over the rails you already use — fiat or stablecoin or crypto, the operator's choice, swappable underneath.

One interface

Over the rails you already have

patala doesn't replace the rails — it gives every product one honest way to move value across them, with the settlement layer left to the operator.

Honest status: foundational — the core, the rails (two crypto, one Hyperswitch adapter, twenty direct fiat processors) and the polyglot layer are built and pass 167 offline tests in the default build plus 547 more with every processor compiled in (clippy-clean, fmt-clean). No rail has been verified against a live network or a live merchant account. Read the repo for exactly what is and isn't proven.
The idea

A substrate, not a platform

Payment platforms sit in the middle, hold your money, and take a cut. patala is the opposite: a thin, sovereign substrate that any product embeds and runs itself, so no one stands between a payer and a payee.

Vendor it

Embed & self-host

Drop patala into your own product and run it on your own infrastructure — Rust core, with a Go layer and more.

Own the keys

You settle

The operator holds the rail credentials and settles directly. patala never becomes the custodian.

Swap freely

Rails are pluggable

Change fiat provider, add a chain, drop a rail — one interface stays, the plumbing underneath moves.
